## Hi, I'm Rami 👋

*AI for things that matter*

I'm a data scientist, Head of AI at [Beamery](https://beamery.com), and a researcher in lifelogging and AI.

- **AI leadership:** I lead the AI function at Beamery, turning data about skills, roles and careers into practical tools for talent and workforce planning.
- **Research:** At Dublin City University I worked on lifelogging systems and helped organise the NTCIR Lifelog evaluation tasks. Earlier, in Grenoble, I worked on automatic image annotation and visual concept detection.
- **Personal projects:** open-source live clocks that show the numbers behind big challenges, second by second, and measure them against the goals we've set:
  - [Emissions Clock](https://ramialbatal.github.io/emissions-clock/) ([code](https://github.com/ramialbatal/emissions-clock)): CO₂ and methane emissions for the world and major emitters, with carbon budget bars.
  - [Renewables Clock](https://ramialbatal.github.io/renewables-clock/) ([code](https://github.com/ramialbatal/renewables-clock)): solar, wind and hydro electricity, and progress toward tripling renewables by 2030.
  - [Jobs Clock](https://ramialbatal.github.io/jobs-clock/) ([code](https://github.com/ramialbatal/jobs-clock)): net new jobs vs people looking for work, and whether job growth keeps up.
  - All built on [clock-core](https://github.com/ramialbatal/clock-core), an open toolkit. Contributions welcome.
